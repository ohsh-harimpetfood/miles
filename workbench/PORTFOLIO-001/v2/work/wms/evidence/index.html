Six assets are Sanitized Evidence. Two are Public Visual Derivatives and are not raw operational evidence.

ITEM CLASSIFICATION DIRECTLY SUPPORTS BOUNDARY
01 · WAREHOUSE MAP ↗ SANITIZED EVIDENCE Physical-digital warehouse mapping · rack-level occupancy visualization · heterogeneous warehouse topology representation Does not establish business impact, ROI, time saving, inventory-accuracy improvement, or productivity improvement.
02 · RACK CARD BULK PRINT ↗ SANITIZED EVIDENCE Managed location identity → physical rack-card · QR-based physical-digital interface · bulk print workflow · Side 1 / Side 2 placement structure Does not establish business impact, time saving, labor reduction, inventory-accuracy improvement, or productivity improvement.
03 · TRANSACTION HISTORY ↗ SANITIZED EVIDENCE Operational transaction history · system-based transaction recording · traceability surface Does not establish quantified effectiveness, perfect traceability, measured time saving, or inventory-accuracy improvement.
04 · ROLE / PERMISSION ↗ SANITIZED EVIDENCE Multi-user role structure · feature-level permission control · operational governance Does not establish cybersecurity certification, enterprise IAM maturity, or quantified business impact.
05 · SIDE 2 MIRRORING ↗ PUBLIC VISUAL DERIVATIVE Public explanation of the operator-view mirroring engineering concept NOT RAW OPERATIONAL EVIDENCE. Does not independently prove operational state or measured impact.
06 · COLD STORAGE OVERVIEW ↗ SANITIZED EVIDENCE Heterogeneous storage topology · container-based storage representation · utilization visualization Does not establish exact live stock, exact container capacity, exact operational quantity, or measured accuracy.
07 · COLD STORAGE DETAIL ↗ PUBLIC VISUAL DERIVATIVE Public explanation of the container internal loading model NOT RAW OPERATIONAL EVIDENCE. Does not prove actual stock state, exact operational quantity, or live utilization.
08 · STOCKTAKE PRINT ↗ SANITIZED EVIDENCE Stocktake / reconciliation · printable physical verification · system-to-physical verification workflow Does not establish measured inventory-accuracy improvement, time saving, labor reduction, or business impact.

  • commercial-equivalent value
