01 / FAILURE VISIBILITY

MAKING OPERATING CONDITIONS VISIBLE.

Logger measurement가 약하게 보이던 실제 operating condition과 intervention transition을 관찰 가능한 사건으로 바꿨습니다.

BOUNDARY
ONE DEMONSTRATED CONDITION · NOT POPULATION-WIDE PERFORMANCE

02 / ANALYTICAL VISIBILITY

COMPARING STABILITY ACROSS CONTAINERS.

여러 CT의 retention과 minimum temperature를 분리해 비교하는 user-facing analysis workflow가 단일 그래프 검토를 cross-container visibility로 확장했습니다.

BOUNDARY
COMPARABLE ANALYSIS · NOT USER VOLUME OR BUSINESS IMPACT

03 / OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE

CONNECTING ANALYSIS TO OPERATIONS.

Current condition, recent trend, maintenance history를 deterministic analysis 뒤에서 contextualize하고 inspection recommendation과 repeated operational briefing으로 연결했습니다.

DETERMINISTIC ANALYSIS→LLM CONTEXT→HUMAN FIELD CHECK

BOUNDARY
LLM-ASSISTED RECOMMENDATION · NOT AUTONOMOUS DIAGNOSIS OR REPAIR

04 / MULTI-CT RISK

REVIEWING RISK ACROSS THE FACILITY.

Longer-horizon multi-CT analysis에서 simultaneous degradation과 operating-pattern instability가 드러나면서 isolated equipment failure만으로 설명하기 어려운 operating / environmental risk를 검토하게 했습니다.

ANALYSIS PERIOD
41 DAYS
SIMULTANEOUS DEGRADATION
20 / 41 — 5+ CT BELOW 80%
MINIMUM-TEMPERATURE TRAP
104 / 529 — MIN ≤ -18℃ / RETENTION < 80%

BOUNDARY
DATASET-SPECIFIC ANALYTICAL CRITERIA · CAUSALITY NOT ESTABLISHED
-15℃, -18℃, 80%, 90% retention are not statutory, legal, product-acceptance, or universal food-safety criteria.

05 / DECISION SUPPORT

FRAMING ENGINEERING ALTERNATIVES.

문제는 어느 CT를 다시 수리할지에서 현행 유지와 반복 정비, 선별 교체와 operating-environment improvement, 통합 냉동창고를 어떻게 비교할지로 확장됐습니다.

BOUNDARY
DECISION FRAMING · NOT OPTION SELECTION, CAPEX APPROVAL, ROI, OR CONSTRUCTION
