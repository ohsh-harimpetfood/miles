05 / TRUTH BOUNDARY

What must not drift

COLD supports

  • temperature data → comparable analysis structure
  • multi-container analytical visibility
  • repeated operational intelligence/reporting workflow
  • maintenance-history-aware contextualization
  • multi-CT operational risk diagnosis
  • engineering / investment alternative framing

COLD does not yet establish

  • cold-storage construction completion
  • selected / approved investment or CAPEX
  • ROI / payback
  • labor / energy saving
  • downtime or failure reduction
  • product-loss reduction / validated quality improvement
  • AI diagnostic accuracy / maintenance success rate
Decision options are framing, not approval: A — Maintain + repeated repair / B — Selected CT replacement + operating-environment improvement / C — Integrated cold-storage facility.
